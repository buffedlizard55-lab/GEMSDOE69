What this lane does

Rather than classifying individual pixels directly, Lane 69 builds a kinematics-resolved fault-tip graph from mapped USGS/INGENIOUS Quaternary fault polylines (trace_segments_utm11.csv joined 1:1 with qfault_attributes.csv for recorded dip direction DIPDIRECT and slip sense RL/LL/N, plus validated DEM hanging-wall polarity). Across every geometrically admissible same-dip step-over (spacing W ∈ [0.1, 15] km, |L| ≤ 3.5W), overlapping flank relay, and free-tip dextral/sinistral termination, the generator proposes structural corridors (oblique connectors, pull-apart rhomb sidewalls, cross-ramp breaches, en échelon strands, and tensile horsetail splays), scores each corridor along its strike normal using upward-continued gravity and reduced-to-pole magnetic horizontal gradients (|∇F · n̂|), 3 km magnetic alteration lows, and 1 m 3DEP lidar scarps, calibrates out-of-fold empirical-Bayes truth probabilities, and selects nodes inside the top corridors via the shared template's greedy marginal-DTI allocator.
