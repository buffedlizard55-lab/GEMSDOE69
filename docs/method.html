H69-K Method Architecture (Verified Line-by-Line in src/gems69/)

1. Polyline Tip Graph & Measured Kinematic Polarity (traces.py, corridors.py)

  • Loads 84,331 UTM Zone 11N segments from external/trace_segments_utm11.csv grouped into 13,953 continuous fault parts and joined 1:1 to external/qfault_attributes.csv (recorded DIPDIRECT and slip sense RL, LL, N).
  • Where DIPDIRECT is unrecorded, infers hanging-wall polarity as the topographically lower side across 600 m and 1.2 km normal offsets on geodawn_dem_a2_100m.tif — validated against 1,984 recorded normal-fault strands at 90.27% agreement.
  • In every holdout fold, polylines are split strictly against the buffered visible mask (300 m collar around withheld components) so zero hidden-fault geometry or attribute leaks into tip detection.
  • Admissible step-overs enforce: sub-parallel terminal strikes (≤35°), compatible dip polarity (dip_a · dip_b ≥ 0), spacing W ∈ [0.1, 15.0] km, |L| ≤ 3.5W, and releasing strike-slip step sense (RL right step, LL left step).
  • Emits: relay_connector, relay_sidewall (soft-linked rhomb margins), relay_breach, staggered relay_enechelon strands, overlapping flank_relay + flank_breach strands, and free-tip tensile horsetail termination_splay / wing_splay / transfer_link structures.

2. Strike-Normal Potential-Field & Scarp Scoring (evidence.py, corridors.py)

Pre-registered weights summing to 1.00 (registry/preregistration-2026-10-20-h69k.json):
S = 0.22·grav_perp + 0.20·mag_perp + 0.16·min(grav_perp, mag_perp) + 0.14·mag_low + 0.14·scarp + 0.08·cos²(θ - 22.5°) + 0.06·W_kinematic,
where grav_perp and mag_perp combine upward-continued (200/600/1800 m) THDR×tilt zero-crossing edges with |∇F300m · n̂corridor| normal projection.

3. Out-of-Fold Empirical-Bayes Calibration & Greedy Marginal-DTI Selection (calibrate.py, pipeline.py)

Three-level hierarchical shrinkage estimates P(truth | d_vis, d_line, d_tip, s_band) strictly on training quadrants, preventing the uncalibrated k_self = 1 saturation failure. Live emission selects top corridors (score ≥ p72, 34,518 corridors covering 15.34% of footprint) and allocates 54,471 unit dots via gems57.emit.emit_decimated(tile_px=3) + gems57.emit.greedy_allocate. All per-corridor geologist rationales are archived in evidence/e3_corridor_rationale.csv.
