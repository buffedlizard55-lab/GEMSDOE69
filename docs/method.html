Method

Tip graph

The pinned catalogue mirror (labels ≡ existing_faults, IR-69-DATA-01) is skeletonized (scikit-image), traced into 3,887 strands between endpoints/branch points, and every strand carries its two tips with terminal strikes (PCA of the tip-adjacent 12-px tail).

Pair acceptance (all lengths in 100 m px)

  • tips from different 8-connected components (same-component pairs are reserved for the hard-link test);
  • terminal strikes sub-parallel, axial Δθ ≤ 30° — the raster carries no dip polarity, so “same dip” is proxied by sub-parallel strike (disclosed limitation IR-69-DIP-01);
  • cross-strike spacing W ∈ [1, 150] px (0.1–15 km);
  • along-strike separation ≤ 3.5W and overlap ≤ 1.0W, computed as interval arithmetic on the strike-axis projection of tip-adjacent strand windows (sign-invariant for axial data).

Corridor emission

  • Oblique connector: tip-to-tip line, extended 0.35W along the local strikes, half-width clamp(W/6, 1, 6) px;
  • En échelon strands (dilatational pairs): 2–8 strands of length clamp(1.5W, 4, 30) px at ±25° to the connector, staggered along it;
  • Pull-apart rhomb (dilatational pairs): rotated ellipse, semi-axes 0.8/0.45 of the tip diagonal; Walker-Lane dextral prior applied through the dilation diagnostic (overlap ≤ 0.5W ⇒ dilatational), because slip sense is unobservable from the raster (recorded per corridor);
  • strongly overlapping steps emit the narrower transfer-overlap corridor.

Corridor scoring

S = 0.20·Egrav + 0.20·Emag + 0.18·Emaglow + 0.15·Elidar + 0.10·Worient + 0.17·Wgeom — weights pre-registered before any holdout look. E-terms are maxima of robust-z means (footprint median/MAD) over multi-scale horizontal gradients (σ = 1, 2, 4 px), tilt-edge |sin(arctan2(VD, HG))| with VD the σ/2σ upward-continuation difference, the provided HG/VG/tilt products, and −tmi/−rtp magnetic lows; Elidar is the max(ex_max, step_max, downface_max) scarp channel; Worient scores the corridor axis against the N–NE axial family best oriented for dilation under WNW extension; Wgeom is the subtype prior (pull-apart 1.0, relay 0.9, transfer-overlap 0.6). Every corridor carries a rationale record (layers with z-values, signature, source) in evidence/e3_receipt.json.

Emission & gate

Propensity P(x) = logistic(1.5(S − S0)) × corridor membership weight; dots placed by the shared template's exact greedy marginal-DTI allocator with truth-size prior |G| = 12,691 px (GEMSDOE32 receipt-derived estimate, cited), restricted ≥2 px from the mapped catalogue. Gate: the corridor arm must beat the identical pipeline with the corridor prior removed (per-pixel physics composite, same layer set, same allocator) on the shared template's spatially-blocked hide-and-recover holdout (seeds 20/21 × four quadrants), paired with 95% CIs. Hard-link validation: for same-component virtual pairs, the linking catalogue mass inside the corridor is masked and recovery within 3 px (300 m) is measured after rebuilding from the remaining catalogue.
