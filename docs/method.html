Method — relay-ramp and pull-apart corridor generator

1. Catalogue polylines and the tip graph

  • Polylines. The INGENIOUS Quaternary-fault export (trace_segments_utm11.csv, 84,331 straight segments) is grouped by record_id and split into continuous parts (13,953 parts from 1,126 records). The join to qfault_attributes.csv (row index = record_id) was verified on slip rate and slip sense for 1,126 / 1,126 records, and the rasterised traces coincide with the competition catalogue raster (99.98 % of catalogue pixels within 100 m of a trace).
  • Dip polarity. Recorded DIPDIRECT where usable (projection on the local trace normal ≥ 0.3); otherwise the hanging wall is taken as the topographically lower side (mean DEM at 600 m and 1.2 km either side). The inference agrees with the recorded dip on 90.3 % of 1,984 comparable polylines.
  • Free tips. Polyline ends with no other trace within 250 m, at least 300 m inside the footprint, on polylines ≥ 300 m long; outward direction from the last 1 km of trace. In holdout runs, tips created by cutting a trace at the withheld/collar mask are excluded (IR-69-08).

2. Corridors (candidate fault traces)

Setting Pairing rule Traces emitted
Relay ramp (normal / unknown sense) facing tips (outward directions antiparallel within 35°), same dip polarity, W 0.1–15 km, |L| ≤ 3.5 W; up to max_partners = 4 valid partners per tip oblique tip-to-tip connector; breaching links from each tip to the partner trace; 1–3 en échelon strands across the ramp; normal-fault tip continuations (2.5 km) and wing splays (±22° to N–NE dilation strike); flank-relay breaches to the nearest 2 off-component fault traces within 6 km
Pull-apart (RL–RL or LL–LL) facing tips, releasing step (RL right-stepping, LL left-stepping), same W and 3.5 W limits two basin-bounding links, tip-to-tip chord, up to three strands striking 22.5° (perpendicular to WNW extension)
Transfer zone (RL / LL tip) every free strike-slip tip two horsetail splays rotated 20° and 40° into the extensional quadrant (right of the outward direction for RL, left for LL); links to the nearest two normal-fault traces within 6 km ahead / on the extensional side

On the full catalogue the generator emits 7,171 relay pairs (mean width 2.83 km, median 1.92 km; 54.8 % overlapping), 1,932 releasing-step pull-aparts, 1,115 strike-slip terminations, 468 transfer links, 4,515 tip continuations, 4,515 wing splays, and 13,200 flank-relay breaches. The published step-over population (Giddens & Faulds 2025) has mean width 2.8 km and ~50 % overlapping (55.3 % in the western Great Basin) — a consistency check on the geometry. With dline ≤ 8 px (800 m), the corridor band covers 47.8 %–65.5 % of the allowed footprint per quadrant while capturing 84.1 %–95.8 % of withheld truth across holdout folds.

3. Scoring, hierarchical empirical-Bayes calibration, and placement

  • Evidence (none catalogue-derived): multi-scale gravity and magnetic edges (isostatic gravity anomaly and RTP upward-continued to 200 m / 600 m / 1.8 km; rank(THDR) × (1 − |tilt|/90°), max over scales), local magnetic lows (−(RTP − 3 km Gaussian)), lidar scarp channel (0.45 ex_max + 0.35 step_max + 0.20 downface_max, by band name).
  • Orientation: dilation tendency cos²β of each trace under WNW (292.5°) extension (maximal for NNE strikes).
  • Corridor score: equal-weight mean of the five terms (pre-registered, no fitting).
  • 3-level hierarchical empirical-Bayes calibration (src/gems69/calibrate.py): leave-one-quadrant-out empirical-Bayes rate estimation P(withheld truth | d_vis × d_line × d_tip × s_band × e_pix) where 1D distance rate r1(d_vis) shrinks toward global p0 (m_v = 100), 3D geometric corridor rate r_geom(d_vis, d_line, d_tip) shrinks toward r1(d_vis) (m_g = 250), and full 5D rate r_full(d_vis, d_line, d_tip, s_band, e_pix) shrinks toward r_geom (m_f = 350). Quantile edges are fitted strictly on training quadrants only.
  • 300 m kernel-radius decimated placement (src/gems69/pipeline.py): candidates are decimated on 3×3 pixel tiles (tile_px = 3, matching the 300 m triangular kernel radius so adjacent pixels do not stack redundant FP mass inside one kernel disk) and passed to the shared template's emit.greedy_allocate inside the corridor band (s_band > 0), with zero dots on or within 200 m of the catalogue (d_vis > 2 px).

4. Instruments

  • Hide-and-recover holdout (template holdout.buffered_component_draw, two draws): whole 8-connected catalogue components withheld (20 % of each quadrant's catalogue pixels), a 3 px collar removed from every catalogue-derived feature, visible faults masked pixel-exactly, pooled DTI by the template evaluator gems57-pooled-hide-v2 with paired 20 km spatial-block bootstrap.
  • Leakage canary: each feature alone, AUC of withheld-truth vs non-truth pixels (limit 0.90).
  • Hard-link masking test: catalogue links joining two traces are masked; the generator must recover them within 300 m; compared with random-azimuth traces from the same tips.
