RANK 1 · H69-P1-profile-null · NOT TESTED

Connector-profile coherence against a matched lithologic null

  • iso_grav_anom (band 13)
  • tmi (band 14)
  • rtp (band 2)
  • lidar ex_max/step_max/downface_max with valid mask
Physical signature
At fixed stations normal to a source-polyline connector, compare signed gravity/magnetic gradient directions and tilt-zero locations over several smoothing scales with translated, same-host flank profiles. Reward residual along-connector coherence, not the independent maximum amplitude of either field. Derivatives must be physically defined; the current Gaussian-difference proxy is not true upward continuation.
Why an unmapped fault?
A buried breaching fault between genuinely compatible visible tips could produce a spatially continuous basement edge while the exposed flank faults have already been mapped. Matched flank profiles aim to reject a regional lithologic contact that merely crosses the corridor.
What differs from existing code?
scoring_joint.py takes min/max of scalar corridor-mean layer summaries. It has no transverse station profiles, gradient-vector transport or translated lithologic-null test. Earlier queued edge-phase ideas did not implement this nuisance-matched residual.
Named non-fault mimic
An intrusive contact or depositional basin margin can still have the same coherent gravity and magnetic profile. The null is a testable discrimination strategy, not a proof of faulting.
Expected improvement / cost
Highest qualitative priority; benefit unknown until independent holdout. No projected score. Medium; cached field derivatives/profile sampling plus matched controls, CPU only.
Data readiness
Existing feature/LiDAR mirrors are pinned but the full stack is not restored in this checkout. Official GeoDAWN metadata was read; no new geophysical dataset is required. Source-polyline attribute integration and visible-only holdout repair remain prerequisite.

RANK 2 · H69-P2-kinematic-counterfactual · NOT TESTED

Releasing-step versus restraining-step counterfactual

  • INGENIOUS v2 polylines with DIPDIRECT/SLIPSENSE
  • iso_grav_anom and its horizontal gradients
  • tmi/rtp magnetic lows
  • lidar-scarp channel
Physical signature
For verified dextral steps and same-dip normal relays, compare transverse gravity-low plus bounding-edge asymmetry in a releasing geometry against matched restraining geometry with similar W, strike and survey coverage. No classification of releasing steps from unsigned overlap alone.
Why an unmapped fault?
Buried pull-apart margins and transfer splays can connect mapped dextral terminations without a continuous surface scarp. Kinematic compatibility supplies an independent reason to search a catalogue gap rather than replicate an existing trace.
What differs from existing code?
Current subtype/score uses an overlap<=0.5W dilation proxy and a flat pull-apart prior. It has no slip-sense-aware releasing/restraining counterfactual or source-record dip check. The transfer-only arm merely selected the non-dilatational proxy subset.
Named non-fault mimic
An inherited sedimentary basin or compaction margin can form a gravity low with asymmetric flanks without active strike-slip release.
Expected improvement / cost
Potentially strong reduction of false geological labels; second priority because attribute linkage and kinematics cost more than reusing field profiles. No numerical gain claimed. Medium-high; authenticate source polylines, preserve record IDs and attributes while withholding complete segments.
Data readiness
Official GDR 1391 v2 archive and field-definitions listing is public and was checked. Mirror CSVs with DIPDIRECT and SLIPSENSE were retrieved and hash-verified in this review. The official ZIP payload and record_id-to-row join are not newly authenticated, so this is not yet implementation-ready.

RANK 3 · H69-P3-low-neck · NOT TESTED

Magnetic-low neck versus broad basin fill

  • tmi (band 14)
  • rtp (band 2)
  • iso_grav_anom (band 13)
  • gravity horizontal-gradient products
Physical signature
Inside an admissible connector only, fit a local cross-strike magnetic trough plus gravity edge and test whether the trough width and center track the corridor along strike after removal of broad basin-scale trends. Compare with corridor-flank nulls at the same W. Require coverage and scale compatibility with the actual flight-line spacing.
Why an unmapped fault?
A narrow demagnetized or structurally juxtaposed zone might reveal a buried connector below thin cover, whereas broad basin fill generates a spatially diffuse low. Faulting is a possible cause, not a unique explanation, and magnetic lows are not universal alteration signatures.
What differs from existing code?
The baseline and joint scorers use negative TMI/RTP scalar magnitude. They do not fit trough width, centerline continuity or W-scaled residual localization. No radiometric-depletion or conductivity arm is reused.
Named non-fault mimic
A nonmagnetic dike, lithologic sliver or narrow paleochannel may produce a similarly localized magnetic low.
Expected improvement / cost
Uncertain and likely restricted to magnetically contrasting host rocks; lower expected coverage than P1/P2, but useful as a specific falsifiable corridor rationale. Medium; profile fitting and resolution/host-rock controls, no new dataset.
Data readiness
Uses pinned competition field mirrors. Official GeoDAWN survey/terrain-clearance metadata was checked; resampling cannot recover 100 m independent information from 400 m flight lines.

RANK 4 · H69-P4-termination-taper · NOT TESTED

Tip-splay fan with along-strand scarp taper

  • Visible source-polyline termination graph
  • USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM and source coverage/quality metadata
  • gravity/magnetic corridor evidence
Physical signature
Within a termination/transfer corridor, require multiple short scarp strands whose measured transverse relief tapers toward subsidiary tips and whose strike fan is consistent with the parent fault. Reject constant-elevation shoreline arcs and road embankments; evaluate directional scarp polarity rather than channel amplitude alone.
Why an unmapped fault?
Small transfer splays obscured by lacustrine/alluvial deposits or too short for regional mapping may retain subtle fault-tip relief, while mapped parent faults alone do not explain the fan geometry.
What differs from existing code?
Earlier en-echelon proposals sought staggered ridge phase. This requires a source-termination hierarchy and measured along-strand relief taper; the uint8 scarp maximum alone is insufficient and is not a measured displacement.
Named non-fault mimic
Alluvial distributary channels, road-cut fans and pluvial shoreline segments can imitate short tapering scarps.
Expected improvement / cost
Unknown, possibly useful in a small subset of corridors; lowest priority because new high-resolution tile/quality checks would dominate cost. High; terrain mosaicking, relief uncertainty and geomorphic negative controls.
Data readiness
DEFERRED, not viable yet: the official competition page confirms 1m_DEM_links.csv exists, and USGS GeoDAWN confirms coordinated 3DEP acquisition. The competition link table requires login; exact free tile URLs, payloads, AOI intersection and coverage were not obtained in this review. No unverified tile is called obtainable.

Validation plan after blockers

  1. Resolve the literal universal-support registry conflict upstream without silently changing the specified threshold or support definition.
  2. Authenticate available source records; remove hidden segments plus their buffers before every catalogue-derived feature, calibration, negative-control and allowed-pixel mask. Do not compute a flank mask or a canary propensity from the full catalogue.
  3. Freeze the top hypothesis and all transforms/weights in a preregistration, with untouched spatial blocks and an explicit current-best comparator on exactly the same repaired evaluator.
  4. Evaluate every single feature alone using the same visible-only folds. An AUC above 0.90 disqualifies it until leakage is explained; an AUC below that threshold is not proof of no leakage.
  5. Use pooled TP/FP/FN DTI and paired spatial-block bootstrap confidence intervals. Seeds that repeat the same spatial blocks are not independent blocks. Record unique positive counts separately from repeated pixel evaluations.
  6. Require positive lower confidence limits versus both identical no-prior control and the current best. Require a complete hash-bound surface scan before placement, then a complete final-dot registry/local-prior scan and format validation before any download. Stop at the first literal duplicate.
  7. Preserve per-corridor layers, signed signature, source record IDs, nuisance explanation and uncertainty for geological review. A fault prediction is not a demonstrated geothermal vent or reservoir.
  8. At most three experiments or two hours; no real weekly slot without a separate selector and actual authenticated allowance.
Maximize P(Win): protect scarce submission slots. Own the Outcome: publish negative results.
