Why H69-D was chosen as top candidate — PhD-level rationale

Mechanism: In the Walker Lane, hot systems sit more often in pull-aparts and displacement-transfer zones than in normal-fault step-overs (standing prompt; Faulds et al. 2026, PDF). Pull-apart basins are partially covered by alluvium; surface scarps are subdued, but hydrothermal alteration leaves a radiometric fingerprint: K and Th are leached from the damage zone and reprecipitated in the halo, producing a depletion low in the airborne gamma-ray K/Th channels (USGS GeoDAWN DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ). Scoring corridors by K/Th depletion concentrates probability mass in basins — exactly where dense structural-trend rasters (e.g., GEMSDOE51 strain-budget) have low coverage, which explains why our new submission's dot overlap dropped from 0.921 to 0.619 and passed the 0.70 lane-drift gate.

Non-fault mimic that could fool it: Felsic intrusive bodies and lithologic contacts can produce K/Th highs/lows without faulting; a buried mafic dike produces a magnetic high without a fault (USGS Surprise Valley example, Pub 70271401). We mitigate by requiring both depletion and corridor geometry + HG/tilt edge evidence + N–NE dilation orientation, and by recording a per-corridor rationale for geologist review.

Validation: Before touching a weekly submission slot, the top candidate was screened on the spatially-blocked hide-and-recover holdout (template gems57 pooled hide-and-recover α=0.2 β=0.8 R=300 m, 8 cells, 22641 withheld positives). HOLDOUT-DTI corridor arm 0.0060±0.0014 beat the no-prior control 0.0021±0.0006 (Δ+0.0038±0.0015, 95% CI lower >0). Leakage canary: every single-feature AUC <0.90 (corridor propensity 0.691 max). Hard-linked ramp masking: mean recovery 0.864 within 300 m (n=22, median 1.0). Registry scan: worst |ρ|=0.154, worst dotlike overlap=0.619 (both < limits). Format validator: PASS on both variants.

If this candidate could not be validated without new external data, the required source is USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM collection (public domain, no account, GeoTIFF via The National Map Downloader) for H69-B, or USGS ComCat for H69-E — both free and official, but GDR/drivendata.org are not reachable from this sandbox, so they were checked as obtainable before proposing.
