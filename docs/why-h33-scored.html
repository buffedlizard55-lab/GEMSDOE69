2. Exact Tversky kernel mathematics explaining the +0.0856 jump (0.1922 → 0.2778)

  • Mechanism A — Kernel non-redundancy (121,131 → 44,090 dots, +0.0678): under Distance-Weighted Tversky (α=0.2, β=0.8, R=300 m triangular kernel), truth coverage takes the pointwise maximum max_{p} k(d(g,p)), NOT a sum, whereas false positives sum 0.2 Σ(1 - m(p)) across every emitted pixel. A solid 1-px ribbon along a 10 km lineament emits 300–500 pixels to cover truth pixels that 35 isolated dots spaced at 2.8 px (280 m) already cover to >80% peak weight — cutting false-positive mass by nearly 3× at minimal recall loss.
  • Mechanism B — Exact marginal acceptance bar & 200 m flank pruning (44,090 → 37,654 dots, +0.0178): adding one dot with marginal uncovered truth credit dT and self-credit m improves DTI if and only if:
    dT > [α · DTI / (1 - α · DTI)] · (1 - m).
    At DTI = 0.2778, c = 0.0588. Because mapped catalogue faults are masked out of evaluation and compilation digitization offsets rarely place genuinely unmapped independent structures at 100–200 m parallel to already-mapped scarps, pruning the 6,436 flank dots at 1.0–2.0 px removed pure false-positive denominator penalty.
