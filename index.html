What is being tested

Geometry-led corridor discovery

Represent fault segments as a tip graph; form relay-ramp, pull-apart, and transfer-zone corridors from visible catalogue geometry; rank each corridor using multiscale gravity/magnetic gradient and tilt evidence, signed magnetic lows, lidar scarps, and stress orientation. Document a rationale for every proposed corridor.

What the science supports

Structural context, not a score promise

The primary regional study catalogs many normal-fault step-overs and describes a domain-specific Walker Lane contrast: dilational strike-slip settings and transfer geometries matter there. It supports testing this lane, not assuming every geometric gap contains a fault or geothermal system.
