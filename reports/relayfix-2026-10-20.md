## Why did h33-2-b2 score 0.2778, and can we beat it? (evidence-labelled)

* The 0.2778 value remains **USER-REPORTED-NOT-ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED**: no
  organizer receipt in this repo binds that score to the file bytes.
* The file itself was **re-measured this session** from fetched registry bytes
  (`evidence/relayfix_champion_anatomy.json`, sha256 `c55bafc4…`): 37,654
  pixels at exactly 1.0, none on the mapped catalogue, nearest dot 2.24 px
  (224 m) from it, median 19.6 px (1.96 km), 5.8% within 300 m of a mapped
  fault. It is a wide sparse dot field, not a ribbon on known faults.
* Mechanism (official metric re-verified on the problem page fetched
  2026-10-20: distance-weighted Tversky α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m triangular
  kernel): for a binary field, `DTI = T / (0.2·(T+S−M) + 0.8·|G|)`, so a dot
  pays only if its marginal kernel credit on *uncovered hidden* truth clears
  the exact bar `dT > 0.2·DTI·(dT+1−m)`. At DTI≈0.28 that bar is ≈0.056 in the
  simplified `m` reading. The family ladder (solid 121k → dotted 44k →
  flank-pruned 37,654; scores USER-REPORTED) is consistent with (i) dotted
  emission retaining most kernel coverage at ~⅓ the mass and (ii) removal of
  100–200 m catalogue-flank dots whose mean credit was below bar — expected
  if scored faults are new expert picks masked against the known catalogue
  and rarely run within 100–200 m of published traces. This is a consistency
  explanation from measured bytes + metric algebra, not a causal experiment
  on hidden truth.
* Can a unique submission beat 0.2778? **Not demonstrated here.** The public
  leaderboard fetched 2026-10-20 is led at **0.4413** (xiaofanhu;
  ORGANIZER-LEADERBOARD), so far better-ranked fields exist; the only large
  lever is credit-per-dot (ranking), not more thinning of H33's own dots
  (which this protocol forbids as non-unique). Our candidate beat its
  same-evaluator holdout comparators (below) — a screen with documented weak
  cross-family calibration (ρ≈0.51), never a projected score. Only an
  organizer receipt from a selector-authorized slot could answer the
  leaderboard question.
