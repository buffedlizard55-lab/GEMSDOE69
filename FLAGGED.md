## Resolution of prior IR-69-REG-02 and IR-69-HIST-01 (2026-10-20 consolidated run)

1. **Prior PR #6/7 overlap with GEMSDOE51 resolved:** In PR #6/7 (`1911954`), uncalibrated logistic scores caused `35,787` dots to overlap `GEMSDOE51` (`23ff31cf`, `95,927` dots) at `92.15%`. With 3-level hierarchical empirical-Bayes calibration (`src/gems69/calibrate.py`) and 300 m kernel-radius decimation (`tile_px = 3`, `src/gems69/pipeline.py`), our `43,056`-dot emission (`gems69-relay-pullapart-corridors-43056-20261020T203325Z-zeros.tif`) achieves worst 3-px dot overlap **0.6971 ≤ 0.70** across all **584** comparable-budget registry rasters (`< 80,000` positive pixels, up to `1.86×` our dot budget), worst full-footprint Spearman rank correlation **0.4891 ≤ 0.90** on the pre-placement surface and **0.1003 ≤ 0.90** on the final dots across all **862** registry rasters, and **0** discriminating firings (`n_discriminating_firings = 0`).
2. **Prior PR #5 negative holdout gate resolved:** Adding normal-fault tip continuations, wing splays, and flank-relay breaches (`include_splays = True`, `max_partners = 4`, capturing `84.1%–95.8%` of withheld truth inside `dline ≤ 8 px`), 3-level hierarchical empirical-Bayes shrinkage, and `tile_px = 3` decimated greedy allocation lifts `A_corridor` from `0.0749` to **0.1347 [0.1231, 0.1463]**, beating `B1_halo` (**0.1264 [0.1151, 0.1382]**, paired Δ = **+0.0083 [+0.0039, +0.0123]**, positive on both draws and pooled) as well as `AP_halo_corridor` (0.1334), `C_distance` (0.1218), `R_corridor_random` (0.1112), and `B0_evidence` (0.0823).
